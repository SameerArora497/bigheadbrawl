How a match plays out

Every online match has eight players on one small map. Play SOLO, every player for themselves, DUO in four teams of two, or SQUAD, four against four. Every match counts toward your rank.

  1. 1

    Pick a mode, hit Start

    Choose SOLO, DUO or SQUAD, alone or with your party. Start drops you into a room's map, or into a match that has just begun. Bots take any places nobody has, so every match has eight.

  2. 2

    Race to 40 kills

    Warm up in your own zone while the countdown runs. Then the walls drop and the first player or team to 40 kills wins. Streaks and MVP land on the final scoreboard.

  3. 3

    Earn RP, rank up

    Every online match earns ranked points, win or lose, and RP never goes down. Climb from Rookie to Champion in each mode over the season.

Squad up with friends

Add friends by their Player ID and invite them to a party of up to four. You stand together in the lobby, talk before the match, and always play on the same team.

Talk to everyone, or just your team

In a match, the MIC and SPEAKER buttons each switch between ALL, TEAM and OFF. By default you hear everyone and only your team hears you. Tap a player on the voice bar to mute them.

Friends list

See which friends are online, in a match or offline, and invite them to your party with one tap.

The leader decides

The party leader picks the mode and starts. AUTO-FILL on teams you up with other players; off keeps your team to friends and bots.

Teammates in blue

A blue arrow and name over every teammate, blue dots on the minimap, and your team's score against the best other team.

A rank for every mode

SOLO, DUO and SQUAD each have their own rank, leaderboard and top 100. Your rank, wins and K/D sit on one screen next to the season leaderboard.

Become a Big Head Legend

Reach Champion and break into the season's top 100 to earn the Big Head Legend title. It's yours for good, even if you're pushed out later.

Jump in mid-match

If a match has only just begun, Start drops you into it in a bot's place, so you rarely wait.

Respawn protection

Three seconds of protection after every respawn to find your feet. Firing ends it.

Same rifle for everyone

Everyone carries the same rifle, so wins come down to aim and movement.

Solo challenge

Fight endless waves of bots, with bosses along the way, and try to beat your best wave.

How it's built

Built by Sameer Arora in Unity, for Android first and iPhone next.

Server-checked combat

The server owns ammo, reloads, fire rate and every hit, so the phone can't fake them.

Matchmaking with bot fill

Twelve public rooms across SOLO, DUO and SQUAD, each running one match. Bots fill the places nobody has and hand them over to people who join.

Frame rate first

Renders at 720p by default (up to 1440p) and runs at 60 or 120 FPS, or matches your screen.

Solo on the device

The solo challenge runs on the phone itself, with no game server in the loop.
