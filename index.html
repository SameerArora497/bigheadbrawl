How a match plays out

Every online match is a free-for-all for eight players on one small map. Matches are short, and every one counts toward your rank.

  1. 1

    Hit Start

    Start finds you a room and drops you straight into its map. Bots take any places nobody has, so every match has eight.

  2. 2

    Race to 20 kills

    Warm up in your own corner of the map while the countdown runs. Then the walls drop and it's every player for themselves. Streaks and MVP land on the final scoreboard.

  3. 3

    Earn RP, rank up

    Every online match earns ranked points, win or lose, and RP never goes down. Climb from Rookie to Champion over the season.

Ranked seasons with a top 100

Your rank, wins and K/D sit on one screen next to the season leaderboard. Reach Champion and break into the season's top 100 to earn the Big Head Legend title, yours for good.

Talk to your match

Voice chat with everyone in your online match. Tap MIC to turn your mic on or off, or tap a player to mute them.

Same rifle for everyone

Everyone carries the same rifle, so wins come down to aim and movement.

Solo challenge

Fight endless waves of bots, with bosses along the way, and try to beat your best wave.

Installing the APK

  1. 1

    Tap Download APK on your Android phone.

  2. 2

    Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.

  3. 3

    Install, sign in with Google, and tap Start.

When a new version comes out, the game asks you to update. Download the new APK here and install it over the old one: your account and rank stay.

How it's built

Built by Sameer Arora in Unity, for Android first and iPhone next.

Server-checked combat

The server owns ammo, reloads, fire rate and every hit, so the phone can't fake them.

Matchmaking with bot fill

Twelve public rooms, each running one match. Bots take the places nobody has, so matches start full.

Frame rate first

Renders at 720p by default (up to 1440p) and runs at 60 or 120 FPS, or matches your screen.

Solo on the device

The solo challenge runs on the phone itself, with no game server in the loop.
